## Preamble

Knowledge has never been so accessible, and technology is changing how we learn, create and work. Artificial intelligence opens possibilities that were once hard to imagine.

**Kortexism** is our answer to a simple question: how do we push our limits without losing what gives meaning to our work? It rests on continuous learning, self-improvement and a responsible use of technology.

## Why “Kortex”

The name *Kortex* is inspired by the cerebral cortex, the seat of reasoning, memory, language and imagination. It stands for our ability to learn, to understand and to solve problems.

Technology extends those abilities: it gives us access to more knowledge and lets us achieve what we could not do alone.

> Kortex aims to be a bridge between human intelligence and the possibilities technology offers.

## Our vision of progress

Progress is not measured only by the power of machines. It is also measured by our ability to understand the world better, to pass on what we know and to act responsibly.

We encourage innovation, experimentation and curiosity. But a technical possibility is not, on its own, a sufficient reason.

> Everything that can be done technically deserves to be examined with discernment before it is put into practice.

## The seven principles

<Principles>
  <Principle n="I" title="Continuous learning">
    Knowledge is a path that never ends. Keep learning, question your certainties, deepen your understanding of the world.
  </Principle>
  <Principle n="II" title="Self-improvement">
    Today’s standards do not define everything we can become. Through effort, discipline and the right tools, our abilities grow.
  </Principle>
  <Principle n="III" title="Technology as a means">
    AI and digital tools serve learning, creativity and problem-solving. They are not infallible authorities.
  </Principle>
  <Principle n="IV" title="Intellectual humility">
    The more we learn, the more we see what we do not know. Knowledge should sharpen discernment, not a sense of omnipotence.
  </Principle>
  <Principle n="V" title="Responsibility">
    Technical abilities come with responsibilities: thinking about the consequences of what we build and the people it affects.
  </Principle>
  <Principle n="VI" title="Passing it on">
    Knowledge is not only gathered, it is shared. Explain and teach so that others can grow in turn.
  </Principle>
  <Principle n="VII" title="The common good">
    An innovation is also worth what it brings to others. We favour what solves real problems.
  </Principle>
</Principles>

## Artificial intelligence, a tool

AI makes knowledge easier to reach, supports reasoning and speeds up creation. It also has limits: errors, bias, over-reliance.

We use it actively and critically: working with it without handing it our judgement. The goal is to make everyone more capable and more autonomous.

## Who is a Kortexian?

A Kortexian is not necessarily an engineer or an AI specialist. Above all, a Kortexian is a learner: curious, willing to put knowledge to the test, aware of their responsibilities.

They do not try to look superior. They try to grow, to share what they learn and to put their abilities to useful ends.

> Being a Kortexian does not mean knowing everything. It means staying willing to learn.

## Surpass without losing ourselves

We want to learn more, understand more deeply and use the possibilities of our time to create, to teach and to contribute to the common good.

Yet we do not forget where our abilities come from. Everything we know and everything we build, we owe first to God: without Him, we are nothing.

So we stay humble, and we choose to keep learning.
