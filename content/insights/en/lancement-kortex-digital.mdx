KORTEX DIGITAL started from a simple observation. Many companies, schools and organisations know they need to go digital, but they lack a partner able both to **build** the tools and to **pass on** the skills to use them.

We created KORTEX to be that partner, from Abidjan.

## What we do

Today, KORTEX is organised around three universes:

- **KORTEX DESIGN**: visual identities, logos, brand collateral and motion design.
- **KORTEX AI**: websites, applications and artificial intelligence solutions (agents, automation, assistants).
- **KORTEX TRAINING**: training and coaching so that teams become autonomous.

Every project starts with the same question: what real problem are we going to solve?

## Our first projects

We did not wait to start building. Among our first projects:

- **Telcosanté**, the website of an Ivorian telemedicine platform: teleconsultation, online booking and a mobile app.
- **OISSU CONNECT**, a platform that tracks the performance of school and university athletes.
- **SEV-OIL**, a bilingual website for a fuel distributor in Abidjan and San-Pédro.
- **shaapi**, our own open-source framework, which scaffolds a production-ready FastAPI backend in seconds.

## An open community

We believe knowledge gains value when it is shared. That is why we are launching the **KORTEX Community**: a place for developers, designers and AI enthusiasts who want to learn and build together.

The first project is shaapi. If you know Python and FastAPI, you can contribute right now: everything is explained on our [Careers page](/en/careers).

## What comes next

This journal will be our logbook. We will share our new projects, our methods and what we learn along the way.

Thank you for following us from the very beginning.
